Rules the diagram follows

Data always enters at the base

Each output's lead goes from the box to the bottom of its first strip (1, 4, 7, 10). That strip runs up, the next runs down, the third runs up. No data cable ever climbs the tree.

Jumpers join neighbours

Strip A's top joins strip B's top across the top ring; strip B's bottom joins strip C's bottom across the base ring. Each jumper carries DO→DI, BO→BI and GND. Tie BI to GND only at the chain start.

Power goes in at the bottom

Every strip has its own fused 1.5 mm² two-core cable from the base box, along the ground and round the base ring to the strip's bottom end. No power goes up the mast. Strip power never passes through the Dig-Quad, which only sends data.

Two supplies, one ground

PSU 1 feeds strips 1–6 (outputs LED1–LED2), PSU 2 feeds 7–12 (LED3–LED4), each through its own fuse box. Join the GNDs at one bar. Never join any two +12 V outputs.

LED 0 sits at the bottom

In WLED each strip is its own segment. The down strips (2, 5, 8, 11) are reversed, so every effect runs bottom to top even though the data runs down those strips.

Measure one strip first

Measure one strip at full white. WLED caps each output at about 9,500 mA so each supply stays near 19 A; above about 3.5 A per strip means lowering those caps. Below about 10.5 V at the top end means adding top feeds.
