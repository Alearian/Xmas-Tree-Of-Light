One-strip bench wiring

One 12 V supply feeds the Dig-Quad, and the board passes that 12 V straight through to the strip. This is the board's normal wiring. One strip uses about a third of a single output's 10 A fuse.

Before you connect power
  • Use a 12 V supply rated at least 10 A (120 W). One strip draws 4–7 A at full white (sources disagree), so a 10 A supply can't be overloaded even with the limiter off.
  • Use a supply with a moulded mains plug or IEC lead. An open-frame PSU with bare mains screw terminals needs a proper enclosure and someone qualified to wire it.
  • Check the strip's printed pad labels. This strip's sheet says black = GND, while the design doc says white.

Fuses: one strip draws 4–7 A, so the board's LED1/LED2 fuse and terminal 5's fuse should be 7.5 A (blade/ATO). Check which fuses are fitted. Don't go above 10 A.

Power-up order

  1. Everything unplugged. Wire the start end only (leave terminal 5 for later).
  2. With a multimeter on continuity, check there's no beep between the Dig-Quad + and − inputs. A beep means a short.
  3. Check the polarity at the PSU: V+ to +, V− to −.
  4. Plug in the PSU. The board's AP WLED-AP comes up after 10–60 s. Reconnect with netsh wlan connect name="WLED-AP" if Windows doesn't.
  5. Set brightness low from the app, then try colours and effects.

Measurements (roadmap Stage 1)

  1. Solid white, brightness 255, current limiter off.
  2. Current: clamp meter on the red lead, or a multimeter on its 10 A range in series (wire it in before powering on).
  3. Voltage at the far end between the bare red and GND leads. Below about 10.8 V (a 10 % drop) shows up as dimmer, pinker white.
  4. Add the terminal 5 far-end feed and repeat both readings.
  5. Over about 4.5 A per 6 m means the final build needs 3 PSUs instead of 2.

WLED settings for the prototype

The board still has the full-tree layout (LED1 = 1,080 LEDs). The strip shows the first 300 or 360 of those, so it works without a change. Two things are worth changing before long tests. Both are config changes, so we back up /json/cfg first.

  • Current limiter: it's set to 40 A for the full tree. For the prototype, set it to about 80 % of the PSU (8,000 mA on a 10 A supply). Turn it off only for the full-white measurement.
  • LED count (optional): set LED1 to 300 (or 360) so effects use the whole strip. TreeTool can put the full layout back afterwards.
